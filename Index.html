About Campora

A decade of outfitting people for the outdoors.

Our story

We have supplied camping essentials to outdoor enthusiasts for over ten years. Every product earns its place by working in the field, and our commitment to quality means you can rely on us for every trip.

Our mission

To equip people with reliable, durable and well-designed gear that makes time outdoors better, and the memories from it last longer.

Our vision

To be the first place campers look for premium gear and honest service, and to help more people enjoy the outdoors.
